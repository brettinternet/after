07 / Make the limits visible

No magic green check.

A readable story is not proof.
A passing example is not approval.

Observed

It ran for these inputs.

Keep outputs, environment, and snapshot. Do not generalize to all cases.

Structural

A bounded fact.

A value changed. An export disappeared. Runtime equivalence is not implied.

Inferred

A prediction to inspect.

A model or heuristic expects this result. No run is implied.

Author says

Intent, not verification.

Agent summaries and test claims retain their author-supplied status.

Not checked

The evidence is missing.

Unsupported languages, absent fixtures, and failed setup stay visible.

Stale

Its basis moved.

Keep the earlier observation. Withdraw its applicability to the new state.
